Support & FAQ

Stuck on a page, lost a purchase, or found a bug? Most answers are below. If not, email us and a real human replies.

Contact

Tell us your phone model, whether it's iPhone or Android, and what happened. For purchase problems, include the order number from your Apple or Google receipt. We usually answer within two business days.

Purchases

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the game, go to Settings (or the Shop) and tap Restore Purchases. The game asks the App Store or Google Play what you own and unlocks it again.

  • Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.
  • You need an internet connection for the restore itself. After that, everything works offline.
  • Restore works on any device signed in to that same store account.
What do the Page 7 Pack and the Full Game include?

The Page 7 Pack is an add-on that opens the Space & Arcade page: 12 extra games. The Full Game opens every page right away (all 79 games), removes ads for good and includes all future updates. Both are one-time purchases. There are no subscriptions. Prices are shown in the store.

I bought something but it didn't unlock.

First try Restore Purchases. If the store shows the purchase as pending (for example waiting for a parent's approval), it unlocks once the store confirms it. Still stuck? Email us your order number and we'll sort it out.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you paid. For iPhone, use reportaproblem.apple.com. For Android, open Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history, or see Google Play's refund help page.

Playing

How do new pages unlock?

Page 1 is open from the start. Get an A rank or better on every game on a page, and the next page opens. That's how pages 2 to 6 unlock. Page 7 (Space & Arcade) comes with the Page 7 Pack or the Full Game.

Can't crack one game? Watching an optional reward video lets you try the next locked game once. The Full Game opens every page right away.

How do ranks work?

Every timed challenge ends with a rank: S, A, B or C. The result screen tells you what the next rank needs (for example "S needs 12s"). Your best rank per game is saved.

Does it work offline? Do I need wifi?

Yes, it works offline. Every game plays with no wifi and no data, so it's perfect for planes, subways and waiting rooms. You only need a connection to buy, to restore purchases, and for ads or optional reward videos to load.

What are the Gauntlet and Endless modes?

Gauntlet: games come at you shuffled, back to back, with three lives, and it speeds up every time you clear one. Endless: no clock; the same game keeps getting harder round after round until you crack, and you chase your high score.

Will my progress carry over to a new phone?

Progress (scores, ranks and open pages) is saved only on your device, and there's no account, so it doesn't move to a new phone. Purchases do: use Restore Purchases on the new device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

How do I reset my scores?

Settings → Erase All Scores. It clears best times, ranks and the Gauntlet record, but it keeps your purchases.

Can I turn off sound or music?

Yes. Settings has separate switches for sound effects and menu music, plus an option to show the rules before every game.
