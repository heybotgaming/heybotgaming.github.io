Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026 · Applies to the You Bored? app for iPhone and Android

The short version

  • No account. You never sign up, and we never ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or GPS location.
  • Your progress stays on your device. Scores, ranks, settings and unlocks are saved on your phone and are not sent to us.
  • Ads come from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob receives device and usage information such as your device's advertising ID and IP address. You control ad personalization (see Your choices).
  • Purchases go through Apple or Google. We use RevenueCat to check and restore purchases. We never see your payment card details.
  • The game is not directed at children under 13.

1. Who we are

You Bored? is a mobile game published by HeyBot Gaming ("we", "us"). For data protection laws, we are the controller of the limited information described here. You can reach us at heybotgaming@gmail.com.

2. What the app saves on your device

The game is fully playable offline. It saves the following only on your device, in the app's local storage:

  • game progress: best times and scores, S/A/B/C ranks, which pages are open, Gauntlet and Endless records;
  • settings such as sound, music and haptics;
  • a copy of which purchases you own, so the game opens correctly offline. The Apple or Google store stays the real record, and Restore Purchases refreshes it.

We do not receive this data. Deleting the app deletes it. The in-game option to erase scores clears your progress but keeps your purchases.

If you use the Share button, the app hands a short text or image of your result to your phone's share sheet. It goes only to the app and people you choose. Haptics (vibration) run on the device and do not collect data.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

You Bored? is free because it shows ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited). Ads appear between games and as optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch, for example for an extra life or to try a locked game once. If you buy the Full Game, the app shows no ads.

To serve ads, cap how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects and sends to Google:

  • Device identifiers: the advertising ID (IDFA on iPhone, only if you allow tracking; Android advertising ID on Android) and other app or device identifiers;
  • IP address, which can be used to estimate your general (approximate) location. The app never uses GPS;
  • Ad and product interactions, such as app launches, ad views, taps and video completions;
  • Diagnostics, such as crash logs and performance data, and basic device information (model, operating system, language).

Google uses this information under its own policies. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent and tracking

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the app shows Google's consent message (the User Messaging Platform) before personalized ads are used. You can change your choice later from the app's Settings.
  • iPhone: the app asks for permission under Apple's App Tracking Transparency before the advertising ID is used for tracking. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the game works exactly the same and you still see ads, but they are less tailored to you.
  • The app does not tell AdMob that it is directed at children, and it asks for ads suitable for a teen audience.

4. In-app purchases (Apple, Google and RevenueCat)

Optional one-time purchases (the Page 7 add-on pack and the Full Game) are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their terms and privacy policies. Payment details go to Apple or Google only. We never receive your card number, billing address or store account password.

We use RevenueCat, Inc. as a service provider to check purchases, unlock what you bought and restore purchases on a new device. When the app starts or you buy or restore, RevenueCat receives:

  • a random, anonymous app user ID that RevenueCat creates for this install. It is not your name or email;
  • purchase history: which products you bought, store transaction IDs and receipts, purchase dates, price and currency, and store country;
  • basic app and device information, such as app version, operating system version, device model, language, and the IP address of the connection.

RevenueCat processes this data on our behalf. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy. We use the RevenueCat dashboard to see total sales, not to identify players.

6. Your choices

  • Ad personalization: on iPhone, turn off Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking for You Bored?. On Android, open Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) to reset or delete your advertising ID. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you can also reopen the consent message from the game's Settings.
  • No ads at all: buy the Full Game.
  • Remove local data: uninstall the app.
  • US state privacy laws (such as California's) may treat personalized advertising as "sharing" or "targeted advertising". The controls above work as your opt-out. We also honor a request sent to the contact address below.

7. Your rights, retention and security

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or California law), you can ask to access, delete or correct your personal data, or object to how it is used. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

We have no account system and don't know who you are, so we can only find RevenueCat records that you point us to. Email us the order number from your Apple or Google receipt (Google order numbers start with GPA.), and we will delete the matching RevenueCat customer record within 30 days. Your purchase stays with your Apple or Google account, and Restore Purchases will still work. For data held by Google for advertising, use the controls in section 6 or Google's own privacy tools.

Legal bases (EEA/UK): your consent for personalized ads and tracking; our legitimate interest in showing non-personalized ads and preventing fraud; and performing our contract with you for purchases.

Retention: data on your device stays until you delete the app. Google keeps advertising data under its own retention policies. RevenueCat keeps purchase records while they are needed to provide restore and to meet tax and accounting duties, unless you ask us to delete them.

Transfers and security: Google and RevenueCat may process data in the United States and other countries, under safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses. Data sent by the app is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

8. Children

You Bored? is meant for players aged 13 and up. It is not directed at children under 13, it is not in Apple's Kids Category or Google Play's Designed for Families program, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app and personal information was collected, contact us and we will help delete what we can reach.

9. This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics, ad trackers or third-party scripts, and its fonts and images are served from this site. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical data (such as IP addresses) to keep the service secure; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. The Snack Snatch mini-game saves your best score and sound setting only in your own browser.
